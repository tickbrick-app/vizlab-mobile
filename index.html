VizLab on your phone

Photoreal renders of SketchUp views and photos, made by Google's Nano Banana. Paste the office Google key once to start.

Where do I get it?

Use the office's Google key: the same one VizLab uses on the PC (VizLab › Settings › Start here). Copy it from there, or ask whoever looks after VizLab.

To make a new one: on a computer go to aistudio.google.com, sign in with the practice Google account (with billing set up), choose Get API key, then copy it here.

The key stays on this phone only. Your pictures go only to Google, and only when you press Render.
