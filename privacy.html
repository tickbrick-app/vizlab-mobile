Clarman VizLab: privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Clarman VizLab is an architectural rendering app made for Clarman Architects. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it, including when you connect Google Drive.

Google Drive

If you choose Connect Google Drive, VizLab asks Google for one permission only: to see, create and change the files VizLab itself makes in your Drive (Google's "drive.file" permission). It cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.

VizLab uses that permission only to save your VizLab projects and pictures in a VizLab folder in your own Drive, and to open them again on your other devices. You can disconnect at any time in VizLab's Settings, or remove VizLab's access in your Google Account settings.

Where your information goes

Your projects, pictures and settings are kept on your own device and, if you connect it, in your own Google Drive. VizLab has no servers of its own that collect them, and nothing is sent to Clarman Architects.

When you render or edit a picture, the picture and your words are sent to the AI service you choose (for example Google's Gemini) to make the result, under that service's own terms. Your keys for those services stay on your device.

What VizLab does not do

VizLab does not sell, share or use your information for advertising, and does not use your Google Drive data for anything other than saving and opening your VizLab projects. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
